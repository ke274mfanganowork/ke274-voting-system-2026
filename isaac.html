Overview & Impact

Executive Summary

This project empowers eligible students by providing a simple, intuitive voting experience while delivering real-time transparency for administrators. By digitizing the workflow, we address critical pain points of traditional paper-based elections: speed, accuracy, accessibility, and trust.

The system serves 550 registered voters across 10 leadership positions, providing a complete end-to-end solution from voter verification to result publication.

Helping Voters & Students

📱

Accessibility

Cast ballots from any device—mobile, tablet, or desktop. No need to queue physically. Vote from anywhere, anytime.

🛡️

Integrity

Prevents accidental duplicate voting by locking each KE number after submission. One voter, one vote guaranteed.

Step-by-step interface with visual progress tracking and clear candidate cards. Never feel lost during voting.

03

Helping Election Administrators

Real-Time Transparency

The password-protected admin dashboard automatically aggregates votes as they are submitted. Officials can view live results with visual progress bars, eliminating hours of manual counting and reducing human error.

Administrators have access to comprehensive analytics including voter turnout rates, position-wise distribution, and complete audit trails.

🖨️

Print-Ready Reports

Generate professional, official-looking result reports with a single click using optimized CSS print styles.

🔄

Easy Management

"Reset All" feature provides a clean slate for testing. Data persists via localStorage without complex server infrastructure.

📊

Data Export

Export complete election data to CSV format for further analysis, backup, or integration with other systems.

Improving the Overall Election Process

By digitizing the voting workflow, the project addresses critical pain points of traditional paper-based elections.

Key Improvements

  • Speed & Accuracy: Votes are recorded instantly and securely in the browser's localStorage with structured keys.
  • Eligibility Enforcement: KE number validation (3-digit, range 001–550) ensures only eligible students vote.
  • Inclusivity: Responsive, mobile-first design ensures students with limited computer access can participate via smartphones.
  • Trust: Separation of voter and admin interfaces builds confidence in the system's fairness.
  • Cost Reduction: Eliminates paper, printing, and manual counting costs entirely.

06

Why This Project Was Created

The Problem

Traditional paper-based voting systems often face challenges such as manual counting errors, delayed results, duplicate voting, and limited accessibility. These issues undermine trust in the electoral process and discourage participation.

The Solution

This Online Voting System was developed to modernize and streamline student elections by providing a secure, transparent, and accessible digital platform. It enables eligible students to cast votes electronically while ensuring one-vote-per-user integrity, promoting trust and efficiency in the electoral process.

Mission: To empower every student's voice through technology that is secure, accessible, and transparent.

07

How The Project Was Built

Architecture Overview

The application uses a client-side architecture with four interconnected HTML pages:

  • index.html (Landing & Information)
  • voter.html (Secure Voting Interface)
  • admin.html (Command Center & Analytics)
  • how-it-works.html (User Guide & FAQ)

All pages share a consistent design system and luxury dark aesthetic.

The Voting Flow

1. Verification

Identity check via 3-digit KE number (range 001-550). System validates eligibility before granting access.

2. Selection

Step-by-step candidate selection across 10 positions with progress tracking and manifesto viewing.

3. Confirmation

Review all selections before final submission. Confirmation modal ensures voter intent.

4. Storage

Selections stored in localStorage with keys election_votes_2026 and election_voted_users_2026.

5. Rendering

Admin panel reads localStorage to render live visual progress bars and calculate percentages.
