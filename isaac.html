How Voting Works

Follow these simple steps to cast your vote securely and confidently in the ISAAC Voting System.

1
📧

Receive Your Voter ID

Eligible students receive their assigned voter ID through official channels. Keep this ID private and secure — it's your key to accessing the voting portal. Never share your voter ID with others.

2
🌐

Open the Voter Portal

Visit the ISAAC Voting website and click the Portal button, or navigate directly to voter.html. This is your gateway to the secure voting system.

3
🔐

Enter Your Voter ID

Input your assigned voter ID to access the voting dashboard. The system will verify your credentials and grant you access to the active elections.

4
👥

Review the Candidates

Browse through candidate profiles, read their manifestos, and learn about their proposed reforms. Take your time to make an informed decision.

5
✏️

Make Your Choice

Select your preferred candidate for each position. The system will guide you through each office, ensuring you don't miss any important votes.

6
👀

Review and Submit

Carefully review your selections before final submission. Double-check that you've voted for all positions you intended to vote for.

7
✅

Confirmation

Receive a confirmation screen showing that your vote has been successfully recorded. Your vote is now secure and counted in the election results.

Responsible Voting Practices

Follow these guidelines to ensure a secure and fair voting experience.

🔒

Keep Your Voter ID Private

Your voter ID is personal and should never be shared with others. This ensures only you can cast your vote.

Use the Official Portal

Always access the voting system through the official ISAAC Voting website to avoid phishing attempts.

Research Candidates

Review candidate information and manifestos carefully before making your voting decisions.

⏰

Vote During Active Period

Cast your vote within the designated election timeframe. Late votes cannot be accepted.

Ask for Help

If you experience any difficulties, contact the authorized election administrator for assistance.

✓

Follow Election Rules

Adhere to all election guidelines and instructions displayed in the voting portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the voting process.

You can access the voter portal by clicking the Portal button on the ISAAC Voting homepage, or by navigating directly to voter.html. Make sure you have your voter ID ready before accessing the portal.

Contact your election administrator or visit the student services office with valid identification. They can verify your identity and provide your voter credentials through a secure recovery process.

Once you access the voter portal with your voter ID, you'll see a list of all candidates running for each position. You can view their profiles, read their manifestos, and learn about their proposed reforms before making your selection.

For security and audit integrity, votes cannot be changed once submitted. This ensures the integrity of the election process. However, you can review all your selections carefully before final submission in the confirmation screen.

First, try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. If the issue persists, contact the election administrator immediately. Make sure you're using a supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge) and have a stable internet connection.

For any voting-related questions or technical issues, contact the authorized election administrator or visit the student services office. They are available to help ensure every eligible voter can participate successfully.
